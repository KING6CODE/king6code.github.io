Privacy Policy — Block Thud!

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Data collected

Block Thud! itself does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your game data (best score, progress, preferences) is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

The app loads display fonts (Fredoka, Nunito Sans, Lilita One) from Google Fonts when an Internet connection is available; this request exposes your IP address to Google's font servers. These fonts are provided under the SIL Open Font License.

2. Advertising

Block Thud! displays ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect data for advertising purposes (advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, device diagnostics), in accordance with Google's own policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete/Reset advertising ID). Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Permissions used

• Internet: to load advertisements
• Vibration: optional haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled in the game settings)

4. Children

Block Thud! is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.
